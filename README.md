# ai-heart-attack-prediction
Exploratory analysis of AI for heart attack risk prediction in healthcare, using a synthetic Kaggle dataset, data visualization and KNN classification. University research paper (UFC).

Undergraduate paper reviewing AI applications in cardiology and exploring a synthetic Kaggle dataset of 8 clinical variables. After EDA and preprocessing, a KNN model classified patient profiles into 4 groups (88.5% accuracy). Limitations: synthetic data and no real outcome variable, so results are didactic, not clinical.
